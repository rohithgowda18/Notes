## 🎯 Recommended Study Order

1. **Layer Hierarchy**: Study the 7-Layer OSI & 4-Layer TCP/IP model in [Comprehensive Guide](./01-Comprehensive-Guide/README.md).
2. **Transport Layer**: Deep dive into TCP vs UDP, 3-Way Handshake, 4-Way Termination, Flow Control (Sliding Window), and Congestion Control.
3. **Application & Web**: Understand HTTP/1.1 vs HTTP/2 vs HTTP/3, HTTPS/TLS Handshake, DNS Hierarchy, Cookies vs Sessions, WebSockets vs SSE.
4. **Classic Interview Problem**: Read the step-by-step breakdown: *"What happens when you type `google.com` into your browser?"* in [Placement Notes](./02-Placement-Notes/Computer%20Networks%20-%20Placement%20Notes.md).
5. **Interview Revision**: Practice with the question banks in [03-PDFs-and-Questions](./03-PDFs-and-Questions/).
