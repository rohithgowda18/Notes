1. **Foundations**: Review [OS Full Notes](./OS_Full_Notes.pdf) for Process vs Thread, PCB, Context Switching, Scheduling Algorithms (FCFS, SJF, Round Robin), Synchronization (Mutex vs Semaphore, Critical Section, Deadlock conditions & Banker's algorithm).
2. **Memory Management**: Virtual Memory, Paging, Segmentation, Page Faults, and Page Replacement Algorithms (LRU, FIFO, Optimal).
3. **Interview Q&A**: Practice [Operating Systems 30 Interview Questions](./Operating%20Systems%2030%20Interview%20Questions.pdf) and [Operating System Interview Questions](./Operating-System-Interview-Questions.pdf).
4. **Final Sprint**: Skim [Last Minute Notes](./Last%20Minute%20Notes%20-%20Operating%20Systems%20-%20GeeksforGeeks.pdf) 1-2 hours before the interview.
